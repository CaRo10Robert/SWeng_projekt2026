## Signing up and logging in {#registrieren-und-anmelden}

### Who can sign up? {#wer-kann-sich-registrieren}
All students and staff of the university with an address of an approved university domain [Placeholder: e.g. @studmail.hochschule.example, @hochschule.example]. Private addresses (e.g. free email providers) are not possible.

### The confirmation email does not arrive. What can I do? {#die-bestaetigungsmail-kommt-nicht-an-was-tun}
1. Wait a few minutes and check the spam folder of your university mailbox.
2. Check the spelling of the address.
3. Request a new confirmation link on the login page. Older links remain valid until they expire.
4. Unconfirmed accounts are deleted after 7 days; after that you can sign up again.

### I have forgotten my password. {#ich-habe-mein-passwort-vergessen}
Use "Forgot password?" on the login page. You will receive a link that is valid for a limited time.

### Why do I have to wait before I can try again? {#warum-muss-ich-warten-bevor-ich-es-erneut-versuchen-kann}
After several failed attempts there is a short waiting period. This protects your account against automated attacks. Your account is not locked.

## Listings {#anzeigen}

### How do I create a listing? {#wie-erstelle-ich-eine-anzeige}
Choose "Post a listing", enter a title, description, category, condition, price or "free" or "swap" and at least one handover location, and upload up to 8 photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP). Listings are only visible to logged-in members.

### Why is my listing "in review"? {#warum-ist-meine-anzeige-in-pruefung}
A filter has recognised a term from the list of prohibited items. A moderator reviews the listing; only then is it published or rejected (with reasons).

### How long does a listing stay online? {#wie-lange-bleibt-eine-anzeige-online}
Until it expires. Before that you receive a reminder and can extend it. You can pause listings, mark them as reserved or handed over, or delete them at any time.

## Trading safely {#sicher-handeln}

### How do I hand over safely? {#wie-uebergebe-ich-sicher}
Meet in a busy place on campus, check the item on the spot and only pay at the handover.

### What scams are there? {#welche-betrugsmaschen-gibt-es}
- **Payment in advance:** Someone asks for money by bank transfer before you have seen the item. Do not pay.
- **Links in messages:** Supposed payment or shipping pages where you are asked to enter bank or card details. Do not click.
- **Fake payment confirmations:** Screenshots or emails that "prove" a payment. Only hand over once the money has really arrived – ideally in cash at the handover.
- **Other contact channels:** Requests to switch to a messenger immediately. Stay in the CampusMarkt chat until you are sure.

The chat automatically warns about links, bank details and phone numbers. Report suspicious messages.

## Contact and communication {#kontakt-und-kommunikation}

### Can others see my email address? {#sehen-andere-meine-e-mail-adresse}
No. Only if you and the other person in a conversation both confirm "Share contact details" do both see the other's university email address. You can withdraw the consent at any time.

### I am being harassed. What can I do? {#ich-werde-belaestigt-was-kann-ich-tun}
Block the member (they can then no longer message you) and report the message or profile.

## Reporting and appeals {#melden-und-widerspruch}

### How do I report content? {#wie-melde-ich-einen-inhalt}
Via "Report" on the listing, message or profile. Choose a reason and briefly describe the problem. You will receive feedback on the decision.

### My content was removed or my account suspended. Can I appeal? {#mein-inhalt-wurde-entfernt-oder-mein-konto-gesperrt-kann-ich-widersprechen}
Yes. You receive a statement of reasons with the rule violated. Within six months you can appeal in your account or via the point of contact. Another member of the moderation team decides again and informs you of the result with reasons.

## Your data {#ihre-daten}

### How do I get a copy of my data? {#wie-bekomme-ich-eine-kopie-meiner-daten}
In your profile under "Export data". You receive a machine-readable file (JSON).

### How do I delete my account? {#wie-loesche-ich-mein-konto}
In your profile under "Delete account". The deletion takes effect after 14 days. If you log in before then, it is cancelled.

## Accessibility {#barrierefreiheit}

### I have come across a barrier. {#ich-stosse-auf-eine-barriere}
Please report it to us, see the accessibility statement. The platform can be fully operated with the keyboard; press `Tab` to move to the next element, and a "Skip to content" link is at the top of each page.
