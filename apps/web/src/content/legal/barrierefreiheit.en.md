Hochschule für Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft Ludwigshafen (HWG LU) strives to make CampusMarkt HWG LU accessible. The legal bases are the Rhineland-Palatinate state act on equal opportunities for people with disabilities (LGGBehM) and the state regulation on accessible information technology (reference as an exercise value, Q-28) as well as the German Accessible Information Technology Ordinance (BITV 2.0) and the standard EN 301 549, which refers to WCAG 2.1 level AA. Our own goal is WCAG 2.2 level AA.

## Compliance status {#stand-der-vereinbarkeit}

This website is **partially compliant** with the requirements mentioned above.

The assessment is based on a **self-assessment** dated 24 October 2026:

- Automated testing of all pages with axe-core: no violations of the levels "serious" or "critical".
- Automated test of the nine core workflows using only the keyboard (sign up, log in, reset password, search, create a listing including an image, manage a listing, send a message, report, share contact details), including a visible focus.
- Display at a width of 360 pixels without horizontal scrolling.

A **manual review** with screen readers and by people with disabilities is still pending. That is why we only declare "partially compliant".

## Known limitations {#bekannte-einschraenkungen}

- **Image descriptions:** Photos in listings come from members. A separate description per image is not possible; the title of the listing serves as alternative text.
- **File selection when uploading:** The dialog for choosing images is provided by the operating system or browser; its operability is outside our control.
- **Screen readers:** Operation with screen readers (e.g. NVDA, VoiceOver) has not yet been fully tested. In particular, new chat messages are announced, but the order of announcements has not yet been evaluated.
- **Legal texts in Easy Language and sign language** are not available. Exercise assumption (Q-28): an explanation of the main content in Easy Language and German Sign Language will be added.
