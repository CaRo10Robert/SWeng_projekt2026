## 3. Principles {#3-grundsaetze}

- We only process data that the marketplace needs (data minimisation).
- There is **no tracking**, no advertising, no analytics or social media services and no content from third-party servers (fonts, scripts, maps).
- Your email address is **not visible** to other members. It is only shown if you and the other person in a conversation both agree to share contact details.
- Data is not transferred to countries outside the EU or the EEA.

## 5. Recipients {#5-empfaenger}

- **Other members** see your display name, your listings and – in a conversation – your messages. They only see your email address after mutual contact sharing.
- **Moderators** of the university see reported content and, for reported messages, only the excerpt around the reported message. For an appeal against the removal of a message, the reviewers see its original text; every access is logged.
- **Processors:** MusterMail GmbH, Frankfurt am Main (fictitious), for sending emails. A contract under Art. 28 GDPR is in place. Operation is handled by the university computing centre as an internal unit; this is not processing on behalf.
- **Authorities** only where there is a legal obligation (e.g. Art. 18 DSA in case of suspected serious criminal offences against life or limb, reporting to the Federal Central Tax Office under the PStTG).

## 6. Transfers to third countries {#6-uebermittlung-in-drittlaender}

No data is transferred to countries outside the EU or the EEA. The platform does not load content, fonts or scripts from third-party servers and does not use analytics or advertising services. Processors are only engaged if they are based in and process data in the EU.

## 7. Obligation to provide data {#7-pflicht-zur-bereitstellung}

Registration is not possible without a university email address and password, because the platform is only open to members of the university. All other profile information is voluntary.

## 8. Automated decisions {#8-automatisierte-entscheidungen}

There is no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Two automated aids only trigger a provisional measure, which is always decided by a human:

- A **word filter** checks new listings for clearly prohibited terms. If it matches, the listing is not published at first but reviewed by a human.
- Once **three members** have reported a listing, it is **provisionally hidden**. You receive a notice and an email stating that this was triggered automatically; moderation decides with priority.

Only the moderator makes the final decision; there is no automated individual decision under Art. 22 GDPR (exercise assumption Q-28).

## 9. Cookies and local storage {#9-cookies-und-lokale-speicherung}

We only store what is strictly necessary for technical reasons (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). That is why we do not ask for consent and do not show a cookie banner.

| Name / type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Cookie `cm_session` (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite) | keeps you logged in; contains only a random session key | until you log out, at most 30 days |
| Service worker cache (Cache Storage) | stores the app's program files (no personal data) so that it loads quickly and can be installed | until the next version of the app or until you clear the site data |
| `localStorage` entry `cm_locale` | remembers the language (`de` or `en`) you explicitly chose; only set when you switch the language. Strictly necessary because you explicitly requested this setting | until you choose another language or clear the site data |

Apart from this, no `localStorage`, no `sessionStorage` and no IndexedDB are used. Should storage that is not strictly necessary be added later, we will ask for your consent first.

## 10. Your rights {#10-ihre-rechte}

You have the right to

- **access** (Art. 15 GDPR) – directly via "Profile → Export data",
- **rectification** (Art. 16) – directly in your profile,
- **erasure** (Art. 17) – directly via "Profile → Delete account" (with a 14-day cooling-off period),
- **restriction of processing** (Art. 18),
- **data portability** (Art. 20) – by law only for data processed on the basis of consent or a contract; we nevertheless provide the machine-readable export (JSON) for all your data,
- **object** (Art. 21) to processing based on Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular situation.

You can withdraw consent you have given (contact sharing) at any time with effect for the future in the conversation. For all other requests, write to datenschutz@hwg-lu.example.
