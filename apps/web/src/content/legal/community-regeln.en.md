CampusMarkt works because everyone on campus treats each other fairly. These rules supplement the terms of use.

## Prohibited items and content {#verbotene-artikel-und-inhalte}

The following may not be offered or sought:

- **weapons and ammunition**, including blank-firing and airsoft guns as well as prohibited knives (e.g. butterfly knives),
- **drugs** and narcotics, including cannabis,
- **prescription-only medicines** (e.g. Ritalin, tilidine, tramadol),
- **exam materials that enable cheating**: exam and test solutions, questions in advance, ghostwriting ("have your term paper written"),
- **forged or other people's documents**, e.g. student or identity cards,
- stolen goods, plagiarism and counterfeit branded goods,
- live animals,
- content that insults, discriminates, threatens or sexually harasses,
- advertising, chain letters and commercial offers.

A filter recognises clear terms from this list. Such listings are not published immediately but reviewed by a human first. [Placeholder: agree the list with the legal office]

## Communicate fairly {#fair-kommunizieren}

- Stay friendly and objective, even if a deal does not happen.
- Reply when someone writes to you – or mark the listing as reserved or handed over.
- Do not share other people's data or content from conversations without consent.
- If you do not want to be contacted by someone, you can block them.

## Hand over safely {#sicher-uebergeben}

- Meet in busy places on campus, e.g. at the suggested handover locations.
- Only pay at the handover, after you have seen the item. **No payment in advance.**
- Do not click on links in messages to supposed payment or shipping services.
- Never give out bank details, ID details or passwords. The platform never asks for them.
- If something seems suspicious: end the conversation and report it.

## Violations {#verstoesse}

Violations can lead to content being removed and the account being suspended. A word filter holds listings with clearly prohibited terms for review before publication; listings with three reports are provisionally hidden until moderation has decided. Every decision can be appealed (see terms of use, section 8).
