## 1. Scope and provider {#1-geltung-und-anbieter}

These terms govern the use of CampusMarkt HWG LU (the "platform"). The provider is Hochschule für Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft Ludwigshafen (HWG LU) (see legal notice). The **community rules** and the **privacy policy** apply in addition.

## 2. Who may take part {#2-wer-mitmachen-darf}

1. The platform is only open to members of the university: students and staff with an email address of an approved university domain (studmail.hwg-lu.de, hwg-lu.de).
2. Each person may only have one account. The account is not transferable.
3. When membership of the university ends, the right to use the platform also ends.

## 3. Private offers only {#3-nur-private-angebote}

The platform is for swapping, giving away and selling **between private individuals**. Commercial offers, advertising and reselling for profit on a larger scale are not permitted. Anyone who sells regularly may fall under the platform's tax reporting obligation (see privacy policy, DAC7).

## 4. The platform is not a party to any contract {#4-die-plattform-ist-nicht-vertragspartei}

Contracts about items are concluded exclusively between members. The university is not a seller, buyer or intermediary, gives no warranty for items, information or payments and does not process payments. Items are handed over in person on campus.

## 5. Members' obligations {#5-pflichten-der-mitglieder}

Members

- provide true and complete information about their items,
- only upload images for which they hold the necessary rights and do not show identifiable other people without their consent,
- follow the community rules, in particular the list of prohibited items,
- protect their login details and report misuse of their account.

## 6. Rights to content {#6-rechte-an-inhalten}

You keep all rights to your texts and images. You grant the university a simple, free right to store the content on the platform, adapt it technically (e.g. image size) and show it to members. The right applies as long as the content is stored under the deletion concept (see the privacy notice): for the duration of the listing, after removal by moderation up to 12 months as evidence, and in backups up to 28 days. The content is only shown while the listing runs.

## 7. Contact sharing {#7-kontaktfreigabe}

Your email address is not visible to other members. In a conversation, both sides can agree to share contact details; only then do both see the other's university email address. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

## 8. Reporting, moderation and appeals {#8-melden-moderation-und-widerspruch}

1. **Reporting:** Every member can report listings, messages and profiles that violate the law or these terms (notice and action procedure under Art. 16 DSA).
2. **Decision:** The university's moderation reviews every report by a human. It can remove content, warn members or permanently suspend accounts.
3. **Statement of reasons:** Affected persons receive a statement of reasons with the rule violated, for illegal content also the legal ground, the facts, the type of measure and information about the appeal (Art. 17 DSA). Reporters receive the result with reasons and information about their remedies.
4. **Appeal:** Affected persons and reporters can appeal against a decision within **six months** of being notified (Art. 20(1) DSA), in their account under "My notices"; for suspended accounts via the link in the email containing the statement of reasons. Another moderator who was not involved in the first decision and who neither reported the case nor appealed themselves decides again (internal complaint-handling system under Art. 20 DSA). The result is communicated to both sides with reasons.
5. **Criminal offences:** If there are indications of a criminal offence involving a threat to the life or safety of persons, the university informs the competent authorities (Art. 18 DSA).
6. **Automated aids (Art. 14 DSA):** A word filter holds new listings with clearly prohibited terms for review before publication. Once three members have reported a listing, it is provisionally hidden until moderation has decided; the affected person is informed. A human always makes the final decision.
7. **Out-of-court dispute settlement and courts:** Independently of the appeal, you can turn to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body (Art. 21 DSA, e.g. Schlichtungsstelle Digitale Dienste e. V., Musterstadt – fictitious) or go to court.

## 9. Suspensions {#9-sperren}

In the case of serious or repeated violations, an account may be permanently suspended. It is then not possible to register again with the same address.

## 10. Availability and liability {#10-verfuegbarkeit-und-haftung}

The platform is a voluntary service with no entitlement to constant availability. The university is only liable for intent and gross negligence and in the case of injury to life, body or health; state liability under Section 839 BGB in conjunction with Art. 34 GG remains unaffected (exercise assumption Q-28).

## 11. Changes to these terms {#11-aenderungen-dieser-bedingungen}

Changes receive a new version number. The next time you log in, you must accept the new version before you continue to use the platform.

## 12. Ending your use {#12-beenden-der-nutzung}

You can delete your account in your profile at any time. The deletion takes effect after 14 days; if you log in before then, it is cancelled.
