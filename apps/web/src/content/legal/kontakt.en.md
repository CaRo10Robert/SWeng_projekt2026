## Reporting content {#inhalte-melden}

The fastest way to report violations is directly on the listing, the message or the profile via "Report". Moderation reviews reported content promptly. Without an account, you can report via the point of contact above.

## Appeals against moderation decisions {#widerspruch-gegen-moderationsentscheidungen}

You can appeal against a moderation decision (e.g. a removed listing, a suspended account, a rejected report) within six months of being notified: in your account under "My notices" or – if your account is suspended – via the link in the email containing the statement of reasons. Another moderator who was not involved decides again (Art. 20 DSA). Independently of this, out-of-court dispute settlement (Art. 21 DSA, [Placeholder: body]) and the courts are open to you.
